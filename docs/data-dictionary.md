## Quick Reference

- **Cadence fields** — `nextUpdateEta`, `lastCommunicatedAt` drive stakeholder and exec cadence badges.
- **Communication objects** — `milestones` are the unit of stakeholder/exec comms; `updates` remain the raw feed.
- **Impact flags** — `businessImpact`, `customerImpactSummary`, and `riskLevel` surface directly in the Executive view.
- **Audience notes** — `audienceNotes` is optional per-incident guidance for stakeholders/executives.
