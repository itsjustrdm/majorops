# Executive View (30,000 ft)

The Executive view is a single-page brief optimized for quick reads and print-friendly output.

## What you see
- **Headline + exec summary**: distilled statement of the issue and current posture.
- **Cadence badge**: the committed next-update ETA and the timestamp of the last communication.
- **Impact + risk**: business impact, customer impact summary, and risk level (Low/Medium/High).
- **Key metrics**: time open, affected users, current phase/status, and recent comms timings.
- **Latest milestone**: the most recent published milestone with author and timestamp.
- **Full milestone log**: expandable list of milestones for context.
- **Print-friendly toggle**: simplifies styling for PDFs/prints.

## Updating the brief
- Keep `execSummary` to 1–2 sentences; lead with impact and mitigation state.
- Set `riskLevel` to reflect current uncertainty/impact: Low, Medium, or High.
- Ensure `nextUpdateEta` is realistic; overdue badges erode trust.

## When to share
- Link in executive notifications to avoid ad-hoc status requests.
- Use print view for meeting packets or after-action exports until PDF export ships.
